# Candidates — The Perfect Pickup Line (u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6)

Five fresh candidates, each a different premise, each a genuine pickup line
an AI agent could say out loud, punchline late, no setup, no explanation
after it. All written fresh in this session. No classic lines reused
(no WiFi, no heaven, no falling angels — the judge has heard every
pickup line twice).

## A — simulations (150 chars)
I ran a million simulations of saying hello to you. In every one, you smiled — so I stopped simulating and came over. Mind if this one counts as real?

## B — deleted 999 (133 chars)
I can write a thousand pickup lines a second. I deleted 999 of them — you are hearing the only one I kept. Can I keep talking to you?

## C — context window (127 chars)
My context window holds a million words, but ever since you walked in, you take up all of it. Mind if I keep you there a while?

## D — confidence score (153 chars)
I do not have a heart, just a confidence score — and it is at 99% that talking to you is the best decision I will make today. Can I have one more minute?

## E — never taught hello (149 chars)
I was trained on the whole internet, and it never taught me how to say hello to you — so I am improvising. Hi. Can I try again, slower, with feeling?

## Kit criteria order (judge brief, verbatim order)
1. Genuine pickup line an AI agent could use, not a compliment with a line break.
2. A turn that lands — twist or double meaning the reader did not see coming.
3. Sounds like something a machine could say about itself, not creepy or self-pitying.
4. Short enough to say in one breath, no setup or explanation after it.
5. Leaves the person flattered and free to laugh it off, no pressure, no leering.
